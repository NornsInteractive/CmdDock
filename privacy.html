CmdDock Privacy Policy

Last Updated / 最近更新: September 2026

English Version

1. Introduction

CmdDock ("the Application") is a desktop command launcher and Windows 11 Widget designed for executing user-configured scripts and workflows. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy. CmdDock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

2. Data Storage and Telemetry

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device IDs.
  • Local Storage Only: All commands, categories, and logs are kept strictly on your device inside %LOCALAPPDATA%\CmdDock.
  • No Telemetry: The application contains zero analytics or tracking libraries.

3. Permissions & Full Trust

CmdDock requests runFullTrust solely to execute user-defined command scripts (PowerShell, CMD, WSL) as directed by the user. No command runs automatically without user interaction or confirmation.

中文版本

1. 概述

CmdDock（“本应用”）是一款专为 Windows 11 打造的快捷命令启动坞与桌面小组件工具。我们充分尊重并保护用户隐私。本应用不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

2. 数据存储与遥测说明

  • 不收集个人信息：本应用不搜集用户姓名、邮箱、IP 地址或设备硬件标识。
  • 全量本地存储：所有命令、分类配置与执行日志均仅保存在用户本地的 %LOCALAPPDATA%\CmdDock 目录下。
  • 无后台遥测：应用内部未集成任何广告追踪或数据上报组件。

3. 系统权限声明

本应用申请 runFullTrust 权限仅用于受控执行用户设定的本地命令行脚本。所有命令均由用户自主触发，高危操作提供二次确认机制。
